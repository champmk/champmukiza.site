A cluster, not a globe

I added a piece of work that is a simulator of leftover bandwidth, not a picture of one. The homepage card is three sentences. The next page plays three locked examples so you can see why packing free GPUs is not the same as meeting a deadline. It is not a second engine, and it is not a 3D cluster. If you want the tables, the repository is the product.

2026-07-06 · 2b4131a

The lamp becomes a real object

If the lamp is going to sit on my name, it should behave like a lamp. You can grab the shade and aim it now — the head turns on its pivot and the beam goes where you point it. There’s a ball-chain to pull instead of clicking, with a click you can hear. And if you toggle it like a maniac, the bulb burns out; you’ll have to click it to screw in a new one. Objects earn belief through consequences.

2026-07-06 · 8bc0147

The light is real

I killed the intro I had — color panels sweeping across the screen, the kind of opener every showreel uses. Now the page starts dark, the lamp flickers on, and the light itself reveals the site, because that’s what light would do. Same day, I fixed the metaphor’s polarity: dark mode means it’s night and the lamp is on. And first visits now default to the time at my desk in Seattle — you’re not toggling a theme, you’re visiting my room.

2026-06-28 · b60a71a

Seat the lamp on the cap line

The lamp’s base now rests exactly on the cap-tops of CHAMP, in font-relative units, so it sits correctly on every screen and every fallback font. Nobody will consciously notice. That’s the point.

2026-06-27 · e0ee13c

The lamp

A theme toggle is a checkbox wearing a costume. I drew an anglepoise lamp instead — arm, springs, joints, a bulb seated inside the shade — and made it the switch. It flickers before it settles, the way real filaments do. This was the first thing on the site that felt like mine.

2026-06-24 · 7b0c95a

Start over, simply

I retired a 3D portfolio you had to fly through. It was impressive the way a lobby fountain is impressive. In its place: one column of honest HTML, three typefaces, no framework, no build step. A foundation quiet enough that every future addition has to justify itself.
